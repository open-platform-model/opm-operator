| `Ready`: `DependentsRemain` | <!-- ModuleInstance --> | <!-- False, not stalled --> | <!-- The instance provides a TransformerRegistration, and its new render drops a contract other instances still demand; that claim is withheld from apply, the rest applies, and the message names the claim, the contracts and the dependent count. | `ModuleResolved`: `ModuleResolved` | <!-- ModuleInstance --> | <!-- True --> | <!-- The module was acquired and rendered; message "module resolved: <path>@<version>".
